Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started as a freelancer?

Simply create an account, complete your profile, undergo verification, and start applying for projects that match your skills.

Is SkillSync free to use?

Signing up is free. We charge a small commission on successful projects. No hidden fees or monthly subscriptions.

How are payments handled?

Payments are processed securely once work is approved. We support multiple payment methods for global accessibility.

What if there's a dispute?

Our dispute resolution team helps mediate issues between clients and freelancers to ensure fair outcomes.
