Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a SkillSync account?

Click the "Sign Up" button on our homepage, enter your email address and create a password. You'll receive a verification email to confirm your account.

Is SkillSync free to use?

Creating an account and browsing projects is free. We charge a small commission on successful projects. No hidden fees or monthly subscriptions.

How do I find freelance work?

Browse the available projects in your skill category, submit detailed proposals that showcase your expertise, and communicate with clients to secure projects.

How do payments work?

Payments are processed securely once work is approved. We support multiple payment methods and ensure timely payments for completed work.

How do I hire a freelancer?

Post a detailed project description, review proposals from qualified freelancers, conduct interviews, and select the best candidate for your project.

What if there's a dispute?

Our dispute resolution team helps mediate issues between clients and freelancers to ensure fair outcomes for both parties.
