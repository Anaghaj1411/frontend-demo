Before You Contact Us

Check these common questions first

How quickly will you respond to my email?

Response times vary by department. General inquiries typically receive responses within 24-48 hours, while support issues are usually answered within 24 hours.

Do you offer phone support?

Phone support is available for enterprise customers and urgent matters. For most inquiries, we recommend email support for faster and more detailed responses.

Can I schedule a meeting with your team?

Yes, for enterprise partnerships or significant business opportunities, we can arrange meetings. Please email partnerships@skillsync.com with your requirements.

What information should I include in my support request?

Please include your account email, a detailed description of your issue, any error messages you've received, and steps to reproduce the problem if applicable.

How do I report a security issue?

For security concerns, please email security@skillsync.com immediately. We take all security reports seriously and will respond promptly.

Can I visit your offices in person?

Our offices are currently open by appointment only. Please contact us in advance if you'd like to schedule a visit.
